The MacKenzie Scott Gift Structure

📋 BACKGROUND: In late 2020, philanthropist MacKenzie Scott made an unexpected, transformational $10 million one-time gift to United Way of Berks County — separate from and above standard annual campaign contributions. The UW Board of Directors, guided by a special volunteer advisory committee, established three strategies: COVID Response Grants, Impact Grants, and Multi-Year Focused Grants designed to create sustainable community change. Under this portfolio, BCC received a 3-year Focused Grant totaling $678,000 (awarded February 2022).

3. Berks County Government — Contracts, Programs & Referral Pathways

Key Finding: Deep County Integration Across Treatment Courts, Housing, Crisis Services

✅ CONFIRMED: BCC is not merely a county contractor — it is structurally embedded in Berks County's behavioral health, criminal justice, and housing infrastructure. BCC operates as an Integrated Community Wellness Center (ICWC), Certified Community Behavioral Health Clinic (CCBHC), and one of three primary clinical providers for Berks County Treatment Courts.

Contract Value — Not Publicly Itemized

⚠ DOLLAR AMOUNTS UNKNOWN: The Berks County Human Services budget is approximately $16.2 million, but BCC's specific sub-contract allocations are not itemized in public county summary ledgers. County Single Audit Reports confirm BCC as a subrecipient of federal and state pass-through funds, with active compliance reviews and program sub-awards — but specific dollar figures are treated as line items within broader block grant allocations. PA Right-to-Know request required to obtain BCC-specific contract values, MOUs, and payment records.

CCBHC Pilot Designation

✅ CONFIRMED: BCC was chosen as one of the original CCBHC pilot sites when Pennsylvania launched its state-backed behavioral health transformation initiatives under DHS guidelines. This early-adopter status gave BCC a first-mover advantage in accessing enhanced Medicaid Prospective Payment System (PPS) reimbursement rates — the financial engine behind the 50x asset growth (FY2017 $395K → FY2025 $19.8M).

DDAP Regional Role

✅ CONFIRMED: The PA Department of Drug & Alcohol Programs frequently utilizes BCC as a primary regional site for stakeholder engagement and opioid-response program reviews, supported by state-allocated opioid remediation trust funds. BCC serves as both a regulated entity AND a DDAP partner — a dual role that complicates regulatory independence.
