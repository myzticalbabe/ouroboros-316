⚠ PLATFORM DISPARITY NOTE: Glassdoor (3.8/5, 62% recommend) paints a significantly more positive picture than Indeed (2.8/5, 48 reviews). 5. Limitations & Integrity Notes

LimitationImpact
Self-selection bias Disgruntled employees are more likely to post reviews than satisfied ones. 62% Glassdoor recommendation rate suggests not all experiences are negative.
Anonymity Reviews are anonymous and unverifiable. They represent individual perceptions, not documented facts.
No MMJ-specific mentions No review explicitly references medical marijuana policies, abstinence-only protocols, or the algorithm change. The corroboration is environmental (constant change, poor training) rather than direct.
Review recency unknown Platforms don't always date reviews. Some may reflect pre-CCBHC or pre-algorithm-change conditions.
Platform filtering Glassdoor and Indeed may filter or remove reviews that violate guidelines. The full universe of employee sentiment is not captured.
