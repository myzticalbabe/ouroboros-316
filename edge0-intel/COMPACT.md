# The Oxford Compact (named 2026-09-23)

The union of forge, mesh, and audit — three powers, one covenant.

## Members
- **The Oxford ShardFoundry** (edge0-intel): evolves tensor-shard populations
  into champion models. Builds, never executes mesh tasks.
- **The Azazael Neural Mesh**: 226 worms executing triage, monitoring, and
  action. Runs drivers, never grades them.
- **The Audit Office** (this repo's harness: evals, SVI, gates, ledgers,
  integrity, safety posture): measures and certifies. Builds neither genomes
  nor runs worms; it admits or rejects.

## Covenant
1. Separation of powers: forge builds, mesh executes, audit verifies.
   No member absorbs another's role.
2. Drivers cross by endpoint only (providers contract); fitness crosses by
   ledger row only. No shared mutable state between powers.
3. Gates admit in both directions: models enter service through svi gates;
   mesh tasks enter fitness sets through ground-truth provenance.
4. Localhost containment by default; anything beyond is separately authorized.
5. The ledger is the single shared truth; disputes resolve by measurement,
   N>=50, temp 0, seed logged.

## Non-goals
Cleverness in naming is spent. Boring identifiers, complete records.
