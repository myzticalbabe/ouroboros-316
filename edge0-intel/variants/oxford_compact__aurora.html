The Covenant

  1. I 🤝

    Separation of powers: forge builds, mesh executes, audit verifies. No member absorbs another's role.

  2. II ✨

    Drivers cross by endpoint only (providers contract); fitness crosses by ledger row only. No shared mutable state between powers.

  3. III 👏

    Gates admit in both directions: models enter service through svi gates; mesh tasks enter fitness sets through ground-truth provenance.

  4. IV 💪

    Localhost containment by default; anything beyond is separately authorized.

  5. V 🙏

    The ledger is the single shared truth; disputes resolve by measurement, N>=50, temp 0, seed logged.
