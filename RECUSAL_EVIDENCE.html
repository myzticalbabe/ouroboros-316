3. Reading's Ethics Framework — What Required Recusal

Applicable Standards

StandardProvisionApplication to Goodman-Hinnershitz
PA Public Official and Employee Ethics Act 65 Pa.C.S. §1103(a): No public official shall engage in conduct that constitutes a conflict of interest. A conflict exists when the official has a financial interest in a matter before them. If COCA received city funding or if BCC's city funding affected COCA's financial position, Goodman-Hinnershitz had a derivative financial interest through her employment. Recusal would have been required.
Reading City Charter §1201 Establishes Board of Ethics to investigate complaints, render advisory opinions, and enforce ethics provisions. No public advisory opinions were sought on the COCA-Council dual role, based on available records.
Reading Code of Ethics Council members must disclose conflicts and abstain from voting on matters where they have a financial interest. Unclear whether she disclosed her COCA employment as a potential conflict on any specific votes. Meeting minutes would show abstentions if they occurred.
