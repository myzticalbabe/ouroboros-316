Subject: Internal Provider Grievance — Registered Medical-Marijuana Patient — Arbitrary Abstinence-Only Treatment-Plan Change and Threat of Parole Reporting To the Grievance Officer / Client Rights Coordinator: I am filing this internal grievance under Berks Counseling Center's (BCC) client-rights and grievance procedures, as required under 28 Pa. Code Chapter 709, Subchapter C (client rights and operational standards, §§709.21–709.34). I am requesting review, documentation, and corrective action regarding a treatment-condition change that I believe violated my patient rights and the clinical standard of individualized treatment planning. 1) STATEMENT OF THE GRIEVANCE (ALLEGED — my account). I am a registered Pennsylvania medical-marijuana patient (PA Dept of Health certification; [fill: certification duration, e.g., 7 years]) with an accepted treatment relationship at BCC of approximately [fill: 6] years. After a BCC treatment-algorithm change, BCC [fill: date] changed my treatment requirements to abstinence-only substance-use-disorder protocols. I was told that non-compliance with the abstinence-only requirement would be reported to my parole/probation authorities — a threat of parole reporting that would disclose my substance-use-disorder treatment status to a third party without my written consent. 2) WHY THIS IS A GRIEVANCE. (a) Client rights (28 Pa. Code Ch. 709, Subch. C). Medical marijuana is a lawful medicine in Pennsylvania, not an abused substance: 35 P.S. §10231.2103(a) protects registered patients from arrest, prosecution, penalty, or denial of any right or privilege solely for lawful MMJ use; the PA Supreme Court in Gass v. 52nd Judicial District, 232 A.3d 706 (Pa. 2020) barred penalizing registered patients for lawful use. An abstinence-only mandate that classifies my lawful medication as "substance abuse" conflicts with these protections. (b) Individualized treatment planning. A [6]-year accepted relationship followed by a unilateral requirement change demands documented clinical justification and an individualized medical-necessity reassessment (55 Pa. Code Ch. 1223) — not a categorical policy shift driven by an algorithm change. (c) Confidentiality. Threatening to report my SUD-treatment status to parole authorities implicates records confidentiality under 42 CFR Part 2 (42 U.S.C. §290dd-2) and 4 Pa. Code §255.5, which prohibit disclosure without written patient consent or court order. 3) DOCUMENTATION I REQUEST. (1) A copy of the facility's written grievance procedure and my patient-rights notice. (2) The documented clinical justification for the abstinence-only requirement change, including any individualized medical-necessity assessment and any treatment-plan revision records. (3) A copy of my treatment plan and relevant progress notes reflecting the requirement change. (4) Confirmation of whether any disclosure or report to parole/probation authorities has been made or is planned, and the written consent or other legal authority for any such disclosure. (5) A copy of the facility's records-confidentiality (Part 2 / HIPAA) notice and consent forms. 4) REQUESTED RESOLUTION. (1) Written acknowledgment of this grievance within [fill: facility's stated timeline, e.g., 5–10 business days; otherwise 10 business days]. (2) An individualized reassessment of my treatment plan that accounts for my lawful medical-marijuana use and my protected status, with a documented clinical justification. (3) Written assurance of no retaliation for filing this grievance and no disclosure of my treatment status to third parties without my written consent. (4) A written response stating the facility's decision, the reasons, and the next level of review available to me (including the DDAP licensing-complaint channel if the grievance is not resolved to my satisfaction). I am filing this grievance before escalating to the PA Dept of Drug and Alcohol Programs, Bureau of Program Licensure, as required. Please confirm receipt in writing. Thank you for your prompt attention. Sincerely, [Your Name] [Your Contact] [Date]
