LEGAL BASIS FOR THIS REQUEST

The following laws require BCC to provide its Conflict of Interest
Policy upon request:

- IRC Section 6104(d)(1)(A): 501(c)(3) organizations must make
  their Form 990 available for public inspection. The COI policy
  is a required disclosure under Form 990 Part VI, Section B,
  Line 12a-c.

- Treasury Reg Section 301.6104(d)-1: Organizations must provide
  copies of their three most recent Form 990s upon request,
  without charge other than reasonable copying fees. Must respond
  within 30 days for written requests.
