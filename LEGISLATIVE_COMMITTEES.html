Subject: Request for Oversight — Berks Counseling Center (EIN 23-2043478), Reading PA

Dear [Chair/Member]:

I am writing to request a legislative hearing and/or oversight review of Berks Counseling Center (BCC), a 501(c)(3) CCBHC in Reading, PA (EIN 23-2043478). BCC receives $3.94M in SAMHSA funds (H79SM088878), ~94% of its $12.5M revenue from Medicaid/Medicare, and $3.3M from CoCA (a state Drug & Alcohol pass-through).

Governance findings warrant review:
• Board Chair Joseph Plageman Jr. and Director Linda Krause are both Edward Jones employees while BCC holds ~$10.48M in securities (investment income grew 11x in 4 years) — portfolio manager undisclosed.
• Director Missy Orlando sits on the United Way of Berks County board, which granted BCC ~$685K (incl. $678K MacKenzie Scott funds) — a governance/funder interlock.
• Treasurer John Robinson operates his own consulting firm (Robinson & Associates, LLC) while Schedule L reports no related-party transactions.
• Reading City Council member Marcia Goodman-Hinnershitz is Director of Planning at CoCA, BCC's largest non-federal funder ($3.3M).
• FY2025 saw BCC's first deficit in 7 years (-$797K) with all $11.68M in program costs in a single line item.
• ⚠️ ALLEGED (not independently verified): a PA state parolee with a 7-year PA DOH medical-marijuana certification reports BCC imposed an abstinence-only SUD mandate after an algorithm change, enforced under threat of parole arrest — despite lawful MMJ status under 35 P.S. §10231.2103(a) and Gass (232 A.3d 706, Pa. 2020). The Senate Health & Human Services Committee — receiving a related letter — oversees BOTH the MMJ program (PA DOH) and CCBHC funding, the exact intersection of this finding. Full memo: MMJ_PAROLE_RIGHTS.html.

I request: (1) committee inquiry into CCBHC governance/conflict-of-interest standards, (2) referral to the PA Attorney General Charitable Trusts section, and (3) review of state pass-through funding oversight. Full documentation is attached.
