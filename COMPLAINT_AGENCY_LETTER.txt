FORMAL COMPLAINT — BERKS COUNSELING CENTER (EIN 23-2043478)
Plain-Text Version for Agency Submission
Prepared August 1, 2026
================================================================================

To the Berks County Commissioners, Reading City Council, and Berks Counseling
Center Leadership:

I am writing to formally request review and investigation of Berks Counseling
Center (BCC), a 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization (EIN 23-2043478) located at
645 Penn Street, Reading, PA 19601. As a $12.5 million organization that
derives 94% of its revenue from government sources — primarily Medicaid,
Medicare, a $3.94 million federal SAMHSA grant, and federal HUD Continuum of
Care grants — BCC serves some of the most vulnerable residents of Berks
County. With that public funding comes a responsibility for transparency and
accountability.

After extensive review of BCC's IRS Form 990 filings (FY2018-2025), federal
grant databases, county budget documents, and public litigation records, I
have identified the following concerns that warrant investigation:

CONCERN 1: $796,924 Operating Deficit (FY2025)
BCC's revenue has declined $1.41 million (10.1%) over two years, while
expenses grew $1.04 million (8.5%), flipping a $1.65 million surplus into a
$796,924 deficit. This is the first deficit in at least 7 years.

CONCERN 2: Executive Compensation — CFO Paid More Than CEO
CEO Christine Axford: $140,004 base + $5,428 other = $145,432 total (FY2025)
CFO Greg Little: $132,442 base + $17,350 other = $149,792 total (FY2025)
The CFO is paid $4,360 more than the CEO. The CFO's "other compensation"
($17,350) is 3.2x the CEO's ($5,428).

CONCERN 3: No Program Spending Visibility
All $11.68 million in program expenses are reported under a single program
category. There is no breakdown between mental health services, substance use
disorder treatment, crisis intervention, or community outreach.

CONCERN 4: Above-Average Administrative Overhead
Management and general expenses consume 12.0% ($1.59 million) of total
spending — above the ~10% benchmark for behavioral health nonprofits.

CONCERN 5: Potential Conflicts of Interest & Undisclosed Financial Relationships
- Major donors are REDACTED in BCC's public 990 (Schedule B withheld)
- Two board members (Joseph Plageman Jr., Chair, and Linda Krause) work for
  Edward Jones Investments — BCC holds $10.48M in publicly traded securities
  (Schedule D). Investment income grew 11x in 4 years ($40K to $446K).
- Board member Missy Orlando works for United Way of Berks County, which
  granted BCC ~$685,000.
- Council Member Marcia Goodman-Hinnershitz serves as Director of Planning at
  COCA, which provides pass-through funding for BCC's SUD programs.

CONCERN 6: Board Treasurer's Consulting Firm — Verified Registered Entity
John Robinson, BCC Board Treasurer, owns ROBINSON & ASSOCIATES, LLC — verified
as an ACTIVE PA-registered entity (Entity ID #3967553, incorporated July 14,
2010, Wyomissing PA 19610). BCC's Form 990 Schedule L reports ALL-NO
related-party transactions, yet no verification exists that BCC has never
contracted with or paid its own Treasurer's consulting firm.

CONCERN 7: HUD Continuum of Care Leasing Compliance — 24 CFR §578.49
BCC receives ~$500K-600K+ annually in HUD CoC grants for 8 supportive housing
properties. Two "Leasing" components ($396K combined) are subject to 24 CFR
§578.49, which PROHIBITS using CoC Leasing funds on grantee-owned units. BCC
must demonstrate these units are third-party leased.

ADDITIONAL FINDING: BCC Litigation History
In 2015, BCC's managed care organization (Community Care Behavioral Health)
conducted a Fraud, Waste & Abuse audit and attempted to retract $105,486 in
Medicaid funds. BCC sued and won at the PA Superior Court (898 MDA 2017), with
$81,704 in retractions cancelled on procedural grounds.

ADDITIONAL FINDING: Prior DDAP Regulatory Scrutiny (Dec 6, 2023)
On December 6, 2023, the PA Department of Drug and Alcohol Programs (DDAP)
conducted a complaint investigation of BCC (Event 4V0D11, Facility TQ8F6601)
and closed it with BCC found "in compliance" within the scope of that
investigation. (Source: DDAP_INVESTIGATION_EVIDENCE.html — public portal
record retrieved August 1, 2026.)

FUNDING CONTEXT
- Total Revenue (FY2025): $12,494,569
- Program Service Revenue (Medicaid/Medicare/Insurance): $11,778,182 (94.3%)
- SAMHSA CCBHC Grant #H79SM088878: $3,944,396 (awarded Sept 2023, through
  Sept 2027)
- Federal Award Inventory (corrected): 125 federal awards enumerated via
  USASpending.gov
- United Way Multi-Year: ~$685,000
- Investment Income: $446,033 (FY2025) — 11x growth from $40,067 (FY2021)
- HUD Supportive Housing: 8 properties, ~$500K-600K+/year

REQUESTED ACTIONS
1. A detailed program spending breakdown by service line.
2. The unredacted Schedule B (major donors) and explanation of board
   relationships with Edward Jones and United Way.
3. A written explanation of the $796,924 deficit and a sustainability plan.
4. Schedule J detail for executive compensation (CFO paid more than CEO).
5. Public posting of all supervisory agency audit findings (past 5 years).
6. Confirmation that BCC's Conflict of Interest Policy addresses the Edward
   Jones and United Way board member relationships.
7. Verification of whether BCC has ever contracted with or paid Robinson &
   Associates, LLC.
8. Lease agreements or ownership documentation for all 8 HUD CoC-funded
   properties, with confirmation of 24 CFR §578.49 compliance.
9. HUD e-snaps / CoC project component detail for the 8 properties.
10. BCC's documented clinical justification for any abstinence-only treatment
    mandate imposed on registered medical-marijuana patients.
