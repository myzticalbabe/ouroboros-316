⚖️ Legal Basis for This Request

The following laws require BCC to provide its Conflict of Interest Policy upon request:

AuthorityRequirement
IRC §6104(d)(1)(A) 501(c)(3) organizations must make their Form 990 available for public inspection. The COI policy is a required disclosure under Form 990 Part VI, Section B, Line 12a-c.
Treasury Reg §301.6104(d)-1 Organizations must provide copies of their three most recent Form 990s upon request, without charge other than reasonable copying fees. Must respond within 30 days for written requests.
IRS Form 990 Instructions Part VI, Line 12c requires the organization to describe: (1) how it regularly monitors compliance with the COI policy, and (2) whether individuals subject to the policy are required to disclose annually interests that could give rise to conflicts. BCC answered "Yes" to Line 12a (has written COI policy) — the policy itself must be produced upon request.
IRS Form 990 Schedule O If BCC's COI policy includes procedures for addressing conflicts involving board members who work for vendors or funders (e.g., Edward Jones, United Way), those procedures must be described in Schedule O — which is also subject to public disclosure.
IRC §6652(c)(1)(C)-(D) Failure to comply with public disclosure requirements can result in penalties of $20 per day (up to $10,000 per return). Willful failure to permit public inspection is separately penalized under IRC §6685.
15 Pa.C.S. §5508(b) Pennsylvania Nonprofit Corporation Law requires directors to disclose conflicts of interest and mandates that the corporation maintain records of such disclosures. This provides an independent state-law basis for this request, enforceable by the PA Attorney General's Charitable Trusts Section.
