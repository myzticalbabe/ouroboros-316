2. What This Evidence Means — and Does Not Mean

QuestionAnswer
Is "in compliance" proof BCC has no problems? No. A complaint-investigation closure "in compliance" means no regulatory violation was documented within the complaint's scope on that date. It does not certify past/future conduct, the treatment-algorithm concern (ALLEGED, gap #43), billing-practice substance (898 MDA 2017, never adjudicated on the merits), or any matter outside the complaint's scope. The project's own DEEP_DIVE_HARM.html labels the algorithm/coercion claim ALLEGED and separately documents the proven items (conceded physician-absence violation 55 Pa. Code §1223.52, HHS OIG A-02-21-02010 + live series SRS-A-26-025, first deficit in 7 years, $105K Medicaid recoupment dispute, HUD CoC leasing rule 24 CFR §578.49).
Why does the investigation's existence matter? The complaint was serious enough to trigger a state investigation. Regulators do not dispatch complaint investigations for trivial matters. The existence of Event 4V0D11 is itself evidence that at least one complainant (or an agency referral) raised concerns about BCC's licensed operations in 2023 — the same operational era as the conduct now under investigation (algorithm change, MMJ/abstinence-only mandate).
Does it predate the MMJ/parole complainant? Yes. The Dec 6, 2023 investigation predates the current medical-marijuana/parole coercion complainant (gap #43, Session 19). The MMJ-specific abstinence-only concern has not been the subject of a DDAP complaint to date — which is why DDAP_COMPLAINT.html asks BPL to expand the licensing review to patient-rights / coercion / treatment-condition practices (28 Pa. Code Ch. 709 Subch. C).
How is this used in the complaint package? As regulatory-history evidence: (a) BCC has been subject to prior complaint-driven regulatory scrutiny; (b) the "in compliance" closure creates a clean baseline against which the NEW, narrower patient-coercion complaint can be distinguished; (c) combined with the 2015 CCBH FWA audit + 898 MDA 2017 and the 10 federal single audits, it supports the LEGAL_OVERSIGHT_ANGLE.html thesis — many reviews, no published adverse finding, every trigger reactive.
