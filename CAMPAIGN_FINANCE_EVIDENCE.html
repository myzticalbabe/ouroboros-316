2. Marcia Goodman-Hinnershitz — The COCA-Council-BCC Triangle

Dual Role Confirmed

🦉 GOVERNANCE CONFLICT — CONFIRMED: Marcia Goodman-Hinnershitz served on Reading City Council (District 2) for approximately 20 years (stepped down December 2023) while simultaneously employed as Director of Planning & Resource Development for COCA (Council on Chemical Abuse of Berks County). Why This Matters

A council member voting on city matters while her employer (COCA) directs $3.3M to BCC creates a structural governance conflict. If BCC-connected individuals also contributed to her campaigns, the conflict deepens from structural to financial. The COCA-BCC-Council triangle means:

  • COCA funds BCC ($3.3M cumulative)
  • Goodman-Hinnershitz directs COCA planning (allocates those funds)
  • Simultaneously votes on City Council matters affecting BCC
  • Any BCC-connected campaign contributions would close the loop
