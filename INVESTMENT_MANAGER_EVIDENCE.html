3. The Edward Jones-Pershing Connection

Known: Edward Jones Uses Pershing LLC (BNY Mellon) as Clearing Firm

Edward Jones advisors do not self-clear. Most EJ client accounts are held at Pershing LLC (a BNY Mellon company) — Pershing is Edward Jones' standard clearing firm for retail brokerage accounts. If Pershing is identified as BCC's custodian, that would be strong circumstantial evidence that Edward Jones is the advisor — but NOT definitive proof (EJ also has advisory-only and institutional account types that may custody elsewhere, and other advisors also use Pershing).
