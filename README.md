## What This Is

An OSINT/financial governance investigation of Berks Counseling Center, a $12.5M/yr behavioral health nonprofit in Reading, Pennsylvania. BCC derives 94.3% of its revenue from program service reimbursements (primarily Medicaid, Medicare, and commercial insurance) plus a $3.94M federal SAMHSA grant and HUD Continuum of Care grants and manages $10.48M in publicly traded securities.

The investigation identifies governance conflicts — including 22% board control by employees of the same investment firm that may manage the portfolio — a $796,924 FY2025 deficit, executive compensation anomalies, patient-rights concerns, and systemic oversight gaps across five supervisory agencies.

## Key Findings at a Glance

### Financial
- **Revenue:** $12.49M (FY2025), declining 17.6% from FY2021 peak ($15.17M)
- **Deficit:** −$796,924 (FY2025) — first deficit in 7 years
- **Securities Portfolio:** $10.48M (Schedule D, publicly traded)
- **Net Assets:** $19.83M (FY2025)
- **Investment Income:** $446,033, 11.13x growth from $40,067 (FY2021)
- **Federal Awards:** 125 awards, $9.35M total obligation
- **SAMHSA Grant:** $3.94M (expires Sept 2027)

### Governance
- **Board:** 9 members, 8/9 employers confirmed, 1 unconfirmed (James Brooks)
- **Edward Jones Control:** 2 board seats (22%) — Chair Plageman (CRD #5964768) + Krause
- **United Way Loop:** Director Orlando works for $685K funder
- **Treasurer Risk:** John Robinson owns active PA consulting LLC (#3967553) — Schedule L says NO related-party transactions
- **COCA Nexus:** City Council member Goodman-Hinnershitz directs COCA ($3.3M cumulative funder)

### Legal
- **BCC v. Community Care (898 MDA 2017):** $81,704 Medicaid retractions cancelled on procedural grounds; underlying deficiencies conceded
- **DDAP Investigation (Dec 2023):** Event 4V0D11 — BCC found "in compliance" (scope-limited)
- **OIG CCBHC Audit Series (SRS-A-26-025):** Live audit covering BCC's $11.78M/yr Medicaid revenue
