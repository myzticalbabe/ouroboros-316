🔒 Privacy & Safety Protocol

CRITICAL: Witness safety comes first. All outreach must protect witness identities.
  • Never name the original complainant in any outreach — the profile (parolee + 7-year MMJ + 6-year BCC) is narrow enough to identify them
  • Use neutral language — "treatment provider in Berks County" not "BCC" in public posts
  • Signal or encrypted channels for sensitive witness communications (Spotlight PA SecureDrop, Signal)
  • No identifiers in deliverables — CONCERN 8 already redacted from shareable complaint
  • Witnesses control their story — offer anonymous affidavit template, let them decide level of detail
  • Parolee witnesses are at highest risk — BCC can retaliate via supervision conditions. Screen carefully before connecting complainants
