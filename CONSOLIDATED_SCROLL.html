🚨 THE CCBHC PROFIT ENGINE — VERIFIED:

Between FY2020 and FY2021, BCC's total assets jumped from $8.7M to $15.0M — a $6.3M (+72%) increase in a single year.

✅ CAUSE: CCBHC Certification. BCC became a Certified Community Behavioral Health Clinic between FY2020-FY2021. This federal designation pays enhanced, cost-based Medicaid reimbursement rates for integrated behavioral + physical health services. Medicaid billing exploded from ~$8.7M to $13.99M (+$5.3M) while expenses only grew $987K (+12%). The CCBHC model generated $4.5M in surplus in FY2021 alone — it is a highly profitable reimbursement structure.

The SAMHSA CCBHC grant ($3.94M) came later (FY2023) and was a separate expansion award — not the cause of the initial jump. By FY2025, net assets reached $19.8M — a 50x increase from FY2017's $395K, built on 4 years of CCBHC-driven surpluses totaling $12.2M (FY2021—2024).

⚠ THE RISK: This growth was entirely dependent on CCBHC enhanced reimbursement rates. If the CCBHC demonstration program is modified, reduced, or terminated, BCC's revenue model collapses. The SAMHSA grant expires September 2027 — 790 days. Combined with the -4.74% revenue CAGR and first-ever deficit, BCC faces a structural cliff.
